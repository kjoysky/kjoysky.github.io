How I work

Structure first, then the page.

The part I enjoy most is figuring out how a site should be organized before anything gets styled.

Step 1

What should a visitor do?

Every site starts with the one or two actions the client needs: call, book, donate, buy, read. Everything else is arranged around that.

Step 2

Sitemap and wireframes

I plan the pages and navigation and sketch layouts on paper before opening Squarespace.

Step 3

Imagery, type, spacing

Photo selection and editing, typography, color, and white space. Twenty years of painting go straight into this step.

Step 4

Build, review, refine

Build in Squarespace, check on phones, take client feedback, and refine until it is right.

About

Kristina Joy Nelson

I have been designing and building Squarespace websites for clients since 2018, and I paint landscapes and birds for a living. My design foundation is a B.S. in Visualization from the Minneapolis College of Art and Design and twenty years of professional painting.

Canva is my daily tool for client and marketing graphics. I do most photo editing in Procreate and know my way around Illustrator. I taught myself Xcode and Swift from nothing and shipped iPhone apps on the App Store, which is my best evidence that I pick up new tools fast.

Based in Duluth, Minnesota, Central time, set up for remote work.
