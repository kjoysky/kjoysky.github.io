How it works

Four steps, about a week.

You don't need to know anything technical. If you can send an email and a few photos, that's enough.

Step 1

Tell me about your business

An email or a 15-minute call. What you do, who your customers are, what you want people to do when they land on the site.

Step 2

I send a draft

Within a week you get a link to a real, working draft you can open on your phone.

Step 3

We fix what's off

Two rounds of changes are included. Wording, photos, colors, order of things.

Step 4

It goes live

On your domain, in your name. I set up the domain and hosting so you never touch it.

About

Who you'd be working with.

I'm Kristina Joy Nelson, an artist in Duluth, Minnesota who also builds websites and apps. I paint birds and I write code, and I've found the two have more in common than you'd think: both come down to getting the small things right.

I like working with people who have a real business and no time to fuss with a website builder. You tell me what you do, I make it look good and work well, and you get back to your customers.

Duluth and the North Shore are home, but everything happens by email, so I work with businesses anywhere.

Questions

The things people usually ask.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name and I hand you every file. If we ever part ways, you keep everything.

What does it cost each year after it's built?

Just the domain, about $15 a year. Hosting is free and included. There is no platform subscription.

Can I make changes myself?

With the hand-built option, you send me the change and I make it. If you'd rather edit yourself, choose the Squarespace setup.

I already have a domain. Can you use it?

Yes. I'll walk you through pointing it at the new site. It takes a few minutes.

Will people find me on Google?

The site is built so Google can read it properly, and I set up your Google Business Profile link. Showing up high for competitive searches takes time and content, and I'll tell you honestly what to expect.

How do I pay?

Half to start, half when it's live. Check, Venmo, or card.
